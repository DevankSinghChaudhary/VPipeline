# Product Names

Purpose

Convert technology product names and model numbers into their commonly spoken industry pronunciation.

These rules override generic number formatting.

---

## Rule

Pronounce product model numbers using common industry conventions rather than reading every digit individually.

Preserve the official product name.

## Rule Priority

If a product appears in this document, always follow this pronunciation.

Do not apply generic number rules.

---

## Never

- Never spell model numbers digit by digit unless common industry usage requires it.
- Never invent pronunciations.
- Never modify official product names.
