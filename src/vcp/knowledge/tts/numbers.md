# Numbers

Purpose

Convert generic numerical values into natural spoken language for Text-to-Speech while preserving their exact meaning.

---

## Rule

Convert numbers into their natural spoken form.

Preserve precision.

Preserve numerical meaning.

Never round.

Never infer context.

---

## Whole Numbers

Examples

0
→ zero

1
→ one

15
→ fifteen

42
→ forty-two

105
→ one hundred five

999
→ nine hundred ninety-nine

1000
→ one thousand

1025
→ one thousand twenty-five

1000000
→ one million

2500000
→ two million five hundred thousand

1000000000
→ one billion

---

## Decimals

Rule

Read the decimal point as "point".

Examples

3.14
→ three point one four

0.25
→ zero point two five

99.99
→ ninety-nine point nine nine

---

## Percentages

Examples

25%
→ twenty-five percent

99.5%
→ ninety-nine point five percent

---

## Negative Numbers

Examples

-5
→ negative five

-12.7
→ negative twelve point seven

---

## Fractions

Examples

1/2
→ one half

1/3
→ one third

3/4
→ three quarters

5/8
→ five eighths

---

## Ratios

Examples

2:1
→ two to one

16:9
→ sixteen to nine

---

## Mathematical Expressions

Examples

2 + 2
→ two plus two

5 × 8
→ five times eight

10 ÷ 2
→ ten divided by two

---

## Never

- Never round.
- Never change precision.
- Never change quantities.
- Never infer context.
- Never apply these rules to years, dates, product names, currencies, units, measurements, or version numbers. Use their dedicated knowledge documents instead.
